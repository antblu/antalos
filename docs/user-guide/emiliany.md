title: "Emiliany · Use"
description: "Open the Emiliany jewelry storefront."
---

Open [Emiliany](https://emiliany.com) to browse the jewelry storefront, switch language and market preferences, and reach the appointment flow.

The initial hosting configuration retains the demonstration catalog and preview cart. Publishing the site does not enable real payments or Google customer login. Store administrators use the separate Medusa Admin service.

For deployment and access issues, see [Emiliany operations](/admin-guide/emiliany/).
